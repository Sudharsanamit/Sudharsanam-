AWS is leading the way in helping businesses tap into Generative AI — without the complexity. Whether you're building smarter apps, automating content, or boosting customer experiences, AWS has services ready to power your next big idea.

### Key AWS Generative AI Services You Should Know

* **Amazon Bedrock**  
    Access top-tier foundation models (like Anthropic, Cohere, Meta Llama) through simple APIs.  
    *Example:* E-commerce sites auto-generate product descriptions and customer replies.
    
* **Amazon SageMaker**  
    Build, fine-tune, and deploy custom models at scale.  
    *Example:* Healthcare companies summarize patient reports and predict health risks.
    
* **Amazon CodeWhisperer**  
    AI coding companion that suggests real-time code.  
    *Example:* Developers accelerate projects while improving code quality.
    
* **Amazon Polly**  
    Converts text into realistic speech in multiple languages.  
    *Example:* E-learning apps dynamically generate audio lessons.
    
* **Amazon Rekognition**  
    Detects objects, faces, and scenes in images and videos.  
    *Example:* Retailers analyze customer foot traffic and sentiment in stores.
    
* **Amazon Comprehend**  
    Extracts insights from text using NLP (Natural Language Processing).  
    *Example:* Media firms automate article tagging and sentiment analysis.
    
* **Amazon Transcribe**  
    Turns speech into text with high accuracy.  
    *Example:* Customer support centers generate call transcripts automatically.
    
* **Amazon Translate**  
    Fast, high-quality language translation.  
    *Example:* Global apps localize content instantly for users worldwide.
